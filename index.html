General Rules:-

  1. You can navigate to a quiz by using the "Quizzes" option in the navigation bar.
  2. There can be 2 types of questions:-
    MCQs
    • Each question contains 4 options with 1 correct option.
    • Once you click on an option, answer is revealed.
    NATs
    • Each question has an integral answer.
    • Enter the answer and click on save to reveal the answer.
  3. Scores:-
    • For every correct answer, you get +4 points.
    • For every incorrect answer, you get -1 points.
    • For every missed question, you get +0 points.
